May Webinar: May 21, 2026

April 7, 2026 · By Susan Cornish

High School Internships – The Competitive Advantage Most Students Miss

Most students wait until college to gain real-world experience.

That’s a mistake.

In today’s competitive environment, students who act early, through internships, don’t just build resumes… they build clarity, confidence, and direction.

In this webinar, we’ll show families how to help their student:

  • Identify meaningful internship opportunities (even in high school)
  • Develop professional communication skills that stand out
  • Build a resume that reflects real-world value—not just activities
  • Leverage networking to open doors most students never even see

At Pathfinders, we believe career development doesn’t start in college, it starts now.
