Top Colleges and Universities in the Columbus Region

The Ohio State University

One of the largest public universities in the U.S., Ohio State offers more than 200 majors across 15 colleges. Located in the heart of Columbus, it is a world-class research university with strong programs in engineering, business, health sciences, and the arts. With an acceptance rate around 53%, Ohio State attracts students from all 50 states and more than 100 countries.

Capital University

A private university in the Bexley neighborhood of Columbus, Capital University is known for its strong liberal arts programs, nursing school, and the nationally recognized Capital University Law School. With small class sizes and a student-centered approach, Capital is an excellent fit for students who want a personalized college experience.

Columbus State Community College

Columbus State is one of Ohio’s largest community colleges, offering associate degrees, certificates, and workforce training programs. It serves as an affordable entry point for students planning to transfer to a four-year university and provides hands-on career-focused education across more than 70 programs.

Otterbein University

Located in nearby Westerville, Otterbein is a private liberal arts university with strong programs in nursing, education, business, and the performing arts. The campus community is close-knit, and students benefit from experiential learning opportunities and close faculty mentorship.

Ohio Dominican University

A private Catholic university on Columbus’s east side, Ohio Dominican offers a values-centered education with programs in business, education, and the sciences. The university emphasizes community service and leadership development alongside academic excellence.

Franklin University

Franklin University specializes in flexible, career-focused degree programs designed for working adults and nontraditional students. With a strong emphasis on applied learning, Franklin offers undergraduate and graduate programs in business, healthcare, technology, and public administration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About College Planning in Columbus

Talk to an advisor
What makes Pathfinders Advisors different from other college counseling services in Columbus?

Pathfinders combines career exploration with college planning – we don’t just help students pick a college, we help them discover a career direction first. Our personalized, course-based approach includes 1-on-1 advising, self-paced coursework, and financial aid strategy, so families get comprehensive support rather than piecemeal guidance.

When should Columbus families start the college planning process?

We recommend starting as early as 9th grade. Early planning gives students more time to explore career options, build a strong transcript, engage in meaningful extracurriculars, and prepare for standardized tests. However, we work with families at any stage – even juniors and seniors benefit from our structured advising process.

Do you help with financial aid and scholarships for Ohio students?

Absolutely. Financial aid planning is one of our core services. We help families navigate the FAFSA process, identify merit scholarships, understand Ohio-specific aid programs like the Ohio College Opportunity Grant, and build strategies to reduce out-of-pocket college costs.

Is Pathfinders Advisors only for students applying to Ohio colleges?

Not at all. While we have deep knowledge of Ohio schools like Ohio State, Capital University, and more, we guide students in applying to colleges and universities nationwide. Our goal is to find the right fit for each student, whether that’s in Columbus, across Ohio, or anywhere in the country.

How do I get started with Pathfinders Advisors in Columbus?

Getting started is easy – simply book a free strategy call through our website. During this complimentary session, we’ll learn about your student’s goals, discuss your family’s needs, and outline how our advising program can help. There’s no pressure and no obligation.
