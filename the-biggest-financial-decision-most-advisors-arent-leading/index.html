The Biggest Financial Decision Most Advisors Aren’t Leading

July 9, 2026 · By Susan Cornish

Table of Contents

For decades, financial advisors have guided families through life’s biggest financial milestones. Buying a home. Saving for retirement. Protecting against risk. Planning a legacy. Yet there is one major financial decision that often receives far less strategic guidance than it deserves: How a family invests in their child’s education.

For many households, a college education will cost between $150,000 and $400,000. That’s 100% to 140% more than 20 years ago. For families with multiple children, that investment can easily exceed half a million dollars. Despite the size of the commitment, many parents make these decisions based on emotion, rankings, or well-intentioned advice from friends rather than a structured planning process.

As financial advisors, you’ve spent years helping clients accumulate wealth. The next challenge is helping them spend that wealth wisely — and that includes understanding the hidden cost of college without a plan.

College Planning Is No Longer Just About College

Twenty years ago, the path was relatively straightforward. Choose a college. Pick a major. Graduate. Start a career. Today’s reality is very different. Artificial intelligence is transforming industries faster than universities can adapt. Career paths are evolving. Employers are placing greater value on skills and experience alongside degrees. College costs continue to rise, while families increasingly question the Return on Education™ of higher education.

Parents aren’t simply asking, “Which college should my child attend?” They’re asking: Is college the right path? Which major offers the best long-term opportunities? Will my child graduate with meaningful career options? How much debt is too much? How do we balance education expenses with retirement goals? Is the most expensive school really the best investment?

These questions extend well beyond investment management, yet the answers directly impact every financial plan. A thorough review of your college financial guide for parents can help families frame these decisions correctly from the start.

Every College Decision Affects the Financial Plan

Consider how one education decision can ripple through a family’s financial future: delaying retirement to cover tuition, taking on Parent PLUS loans, reducing retirement contributions, selling appreciated investments at an inopportune time, or supporting an adult child who changes majors multiple times or struggles to launch a career.

The right educational decision doesn’t just save money — it can preserve decades of thoughtful financial planning. Understanding family budgeting and FAFSA planning is an essential step toward protecting those hard-earned assets.

Your Clients Don’t Need Another Admissions Consultant

Many families assume they need someone to help complete applications or edit essays. Those services certainly have value. But the bigger opportunity is helping families make better decisions before applications are submitted. The most successful outcomes begin with questions such as: What careers align with this student’s strengths, interests, and personality? Which educational pathways support those goals? Which colleges provide the strongest Return on Education™? How can we minimize unnecessary education costs while maximizing opportunity?

True career exploration should come before college selection — when students understand themselves first, every subsequent decision becomes more intentional and financially sound.

Specialists Strengthen Client Relationships

The most trusted advisors rarely try to become experts in every discipline. They build teams. CPAs partner with estate attorneys. Attorneys work alongside valuation experts. Physicians refer patients to specialists. Financial advisors do the same because clients benefit from coordinated expertise.

Education planning should be viewed through the same lens. Partnering with a trusted education planning specialist allows advisors to remain at the center of the client’s financial life while ensuring families receive expert guidance in an increasingly complex area.

A Better Experience for Your Clients

When education planning is integrated into financial planning, families gain: greater confidence in major financial decisions, better alignment between career goals and educational investments, reduced stress and parent-child conflict during the college planning process, clearer expectations about education costs and long-term financial impact, and increased confidence that education decisions support — not derail — the family’s broader financial goals.

Most importantly, clients recognize that their advisor is looking beyond investments to help them navigate one of life’s most significant financial decisions. Families who understand why financial aid appeals are overlooked often find thousands of dollars left on the table that proper planning could have captured.

How Pathfinders Supports Financial Advisors

At Pathfinders College & Career Advisors, we believe career planning should come before college planning. Our structured process helps students discover careers that fit their strengths, interests, and goals before evaluating educational pathways. We then guide families through college selection, admissions planning, financial considerations, and long-term decision-making.

We don’t sell investments. We don’t replace financial advisors. Instead, we become a trusted extension of your advisory team, helping families make informed education decisions that complement the financial strategy you’ve worked hard to build. Together, we help clients make one of the largest investments of their lives with greater clarity, confidence, and purpose.

Final Thought

The best financial advisors don’t simply help clients grow wealth. They help clients make wise decisions with that wealth. Education planning has become one of the most significant financial decisions families face. By bringing an education planning specialist into your network of trusted professionals, you provide clients with another layer of expertise while reinforcing your role as the advisor who sees the complete picture.

Because protecting a family’s financial future isn’t just about managing assets — it’s about helping them make the right decisions with every dollar they invest, including the ones they invest in their children’s future.
