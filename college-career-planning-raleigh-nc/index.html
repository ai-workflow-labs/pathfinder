Frequently Asked Questions - Raleigh College Planning

Talk to an advisor
When should my Raleigh high schooler start college planning?

We recommend starting career exploration and college planning as early as 9th grade. This gives Raleigh families enough time to explore career paths, build a strong academic profile, and prepare strategically for competitive admissions at Research Triangle schools and beyond.

Do you only help with North Carolina colleges?

Not at all! While we have deep expertise with NC State, Duke, UNC, and other Triangle-area schools, we work with families applying to colleges and universities across the entire United States. Our process starts with career fit – then we build a college list that makes sense nationwide.

How much do your college planning services cost?

We offer several service tiers, from our self-paced online course to comprehensive 1-on-1 advising packages. Book a free strategy call so we can understand your family’s needs and recommend the best fit for your budget and goals.

Can Pathfinders help my student find scholarships?

Yes! Financial planning is a core part of our service. We help Raleigh families identify scholarship opportunities, optimize FAFSA submissions, compare financial aid packages, and develop strategies that can save thousands of dollars on college costs.

What if my teen doesn't know what they want to study?

That’s actually our specialty. More than 70% of students change their major at least once. Our career-first approach uses validated assessments and guided exploration to help your teen discover their strengths and interests before committing to a major – saving time and tuition dollars.
