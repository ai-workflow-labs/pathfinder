Top Colleges & Universities Near Pittsburgh

Pittsburgh boasts some of America’s most respected universities. We help students find the right fit among these institutions and beyond.

University of Pittsburgh

A top-ranked public research university known for its strong programs in medicine, engineering, business, and liberal arts. Pitt offers excellent value for Pennsylvania residents.

Carnegie Mellon University

World-renowned for computer science, engineering, robotics, and fine arts. CMU is one of the most innovative and selective universities in the country.

Duquesne University

A private Catholic university offering strong programs in pharmacy, nursing, business, and music. Located on a blufftop campus overlooking downtown Pittsburgh.

Robert Morris University

A private university in Moon Township with strong programs in business, nursing, engineering, and cybersecurity. Known for personalized attention and career preparation.

Chatham University

A private university in Shadyside known for sustainability programs, health sciences, and its beautiful arboretum campus. Offers a supportive, student-centered environment.

Point Park University

Located in the heart of downtown Pittsburgh, Point Park excels in performing arts, journalism, business, and criminal justice with real-world urban learning opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions About College Planning in Pittsburgh

Talk to an advisor
When should Pittsburgh families start college planning?

We recommend starting as early as 9th grade. Early planning allows time for career exploration, strategic course selection, extracurricular development, and building a strong college-ready profile. However, we work with families at every stage – even juniors and seniors can benefit greatly from our guidance.

Do you only help with Pittsburgh-area colleges?

Not at all! While we have deep knowledge of Western Pennsylvania schools like Pitt, Carnegie Mellon, and Duquesne, we help students explore and apply to colleges nationwide. Our goal is finding the best overall fit regardless of location.

How much does college planning with Pathfinders cost?

We offer a complimentary strategy call so you can learn about our approach and see if we are the right fit. Our planning packages are designed to save families significantly more than they cost through smarter school selection and financial aid optimization.

What makes Pathfinders different from other college consultants?

We take a career-first approach. Most consultants focus on getting into the most prestigious school possible. We focus on finding the right career path first, then matching students with colleges that best prepare them for that career – while minimizing costs.

Can you help with financial aid and scholarships for PA students?

Absolutely. We help Pittsburgh families navigate FAFSA, CSS Profile, PA state grants, institutional merit scholarships, and financial aid negotiations. Many of our families save tens of thousands of dollars through strategic financial planning.
