College Admissions Stress: Even Jenna Bush’s Story Proves Parents Need Help

October 3, 2025 · By Aly

This week, Jenna Bush Hager shared a story that struck a nerve with parents across the country. She recalled how her father, former President George W. Bush, broke down in tears during her college application process. Yes, even the daughter of a U.S. President, someone with every resource imaginable, faced the same stress, confusion, and emotional weight that millions of families feel each year when it comes to college.

If a family with that kind of support struggled, what does that mean for the rest of us? For most parents, the process feels like a storm you didn’t see coming. Deadlines arrive out of nowhere. Essays and test scores start to pile up. And through it all, you’re trying to keep your child confident, motivated, and excited about their future while you’re quietly worrying about the price tag.

The truth is, Jenna’s story isn’t unusual, it’s just one of the few that made national news. Every year, we meet parents who say, “I thought I could handle this on my own, but now we’re lost in the process.” Students miss deadlines because they didn’t have a system in place. Families spend thousands more than necessary because they didn’t know about merit aid or scholarship strategies. Kids apply to schools that don’t truly fit them, only to transfer later, costing families even more time and money.

This is where Pathfinders College & Career Advisors, in partnership with College Tutors, comes in. Our work is built on the belief that no parent should have to go through this alone. We provide the roadmap families wish they’d had from the very beginning. Instead of reacting to stress, we help you take charge of the process with confidence and clarity. Let us help with the Calculus or Chemistry homework, without you being the bad guy.

When you partner with us, here’s what changes: deadlines stop sneaking up on you, because we create a timeline that keeps everyone accountable. The grades go up, and the overwhelming list of colleges shrinks down to the ones that truly fit your child, academically, socially, and financially. Tests and grades stop being last-minute panic and instead become opportunities for your student to shine. We are here to help your high schooler now, get good grades and into a great college for their dream career.

Parents often tell us that working with us gave them back something priceless, peace of mind. Instead of lying awake at night worrying about how to help with Geometry homework or whether their child is on track for college, they feel reassured knowing they have a partner walking beside them. We’re not just advisors; we’re advocates for your child’s future.

So as you think about Jenna Bush Hager’s story, ask yourself: if even a former President found the process overwhelming, why would you try to tackle it alone? You don’t have to. That’s the message we want every family to hear.

High school course work can be stressful, and college is one of the biggest investments you will ever make in your child’s life. It’s too important to leave to chance, too costly to navigate blindly, and too stressful to carry alone. The right guidance can make all the difference, not just in where your child goes to school, but in how your entire family experiences the journey.

Parents, this is your call to action. Don’t let your family be the next story of stress, tears, and missed opportunities. Reach out today to schedule a consultation with Pathfinders College and Career Advisors and College Tutors. Together, we’ll build a plan that takes the pressure off your shoulders and puts your student on the path to success.

It doesn’t have to be this way. Let us help you.
