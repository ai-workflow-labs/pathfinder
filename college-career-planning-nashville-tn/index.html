Top Colleges Near Nashville, Tennessee

Middle Tennessee offers an impressive range of colleges and universities. Here are some of the top institutions Nashville-area students consider:

Vanderbilt University

A top-15 national research university in the heart of Nashville, known for world-class programs in medicine, engineering, education, and liberal arts with generous financial-aid packages.

Belmont University

A premier private university in Nashville’s Music Row district, offering strong business, music, nursing, and pharmacy programs with a vibrant campus community.

Tennessee State University

A historically Black public university offering affordable education with strong programs in engineering, business, and health sciences, plus robust scholarship opportunities.

Lipscomb University

A private Christian university in Green Hills known for personalized education, strong pre-professional programs, and a 96% career-placement rate within six months of graduation.

Middle Tennessee State University

The largest undergraduate university in Tennessee, located in Murfreesboro, offering 300+ programs with nationally recognized aerospace, recording industry, and business programs.

University of Tennessee, Knoxville

Tennessee’s flagship public research university, just three hours east of Nashville, offering top-ranked programs in engineering, business, and supply chain management with competitive in-state tuition.

Frequently Asked Questions About College Planning in Nashville

Talk to an advisor
When should Nashville families start college planning?

We recommend starting career exploration and college planning in 9th or 10th grade. This gives students time to explore interests, build a strong academic profile, and take advantage of summer programs and extracurriculars at Nashville-area institutions.

What Tennessee scholarship programs should we know about?

Tennessee offers several valuable scholarship programs including the HOPE Scholarship (based on GPA and ACT/SAT scores), Tennessee Promise (free community college tuition), and the Tennessee Reconnect grant. We help families understand eligibility and maximize these opportunities.

How much does a college admissions consultant cost in Nashville?

Fees vary widely, but most comprehensive college planning services range from $1,500 to $6,000 depending on the level of support. At Pathfinders, we offer flexible packages including a self-paced course option that makes expert guidance accessible to more families.

How do you help families reduce college costs?

We help families understand the true cost of attendance, compare financial-aid award letters, identify merit scholarship opportunities, and develop a strategy that can save tens of thousands of dollars over four years.

Can you help if my student doesn't know what they want to study?

Absolutely – that’s where we start! Our career-exploration process uses validated assessments and guided discovery to help students identify career paths that match their strengths and interests before selecting a major or college.
