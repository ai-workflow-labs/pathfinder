Aly Foster

My Story

Aly holds a degree in business administration and has spent more than sixteen years guiding parents and their children through big decisions. Her focus is always on what’s right for each student and each family, not on a one-size-fits-all answer.

Her path to that work wasn’t a straight line. Aly started college without a clear plan. She first considered nursing, then switched to business. She even moved across the country to a new college for the new major. She came out of it with a career she loves, but she also knows how much time, money and stress earlier career clarity could have saved her.

That experience is why she believes in Pathfinders’ approach: career first, college second. When a student understands who they want to become, each decision after that feels less like a guess and more like a plan.

Aly makes sure every family gets a clear, organized and personal experience from the first conversation to the last. Her favorite part of the work is watching parents go from feeling overwhelmed to feeling confident, and seeing students get excited about their first job offer…not just their college acceptances.
