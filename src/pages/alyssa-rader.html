My Story

Alyssa Rader understands how hard it can be to choose a direction when you know your strengths but aren’t sure where they’ll lead. After earning a degree in Customer Service Leadership, she explored roles in sales, management, and operations while figuring out what kind of career would feel meaningful and fulfilling.

That experience is one reason Alyssa became a college and career advisor with Pathfinders. She wants students to have the guidance she wished she’d had: someone who listens, helps them explore their options, and supports them as they make confident decisions about what comes next.

Alyssa believes students don’t need to have their entire future mapped out. They need confidence in the decisions they’re making today and the freedom to keep exploring. As they learn more about their interests and opportunities, they may refine their plans—and that’s a natural part of finding the right path. With the right support, students can move forward with greater clarity and know they don’t have to navigate the journey alone.
