Introduction

Not sure what you want to do after high school? You’re not alone, but winging it is risky and expensive. Pathfinders College & Career Advisors Boot Camp helps you figure out who you are and where you’re headed before making big decisions about college, trades, or any other path. We’ll help you connect your strengths to real careers, explore your options with experts, and build a game plan that actually makes sense for your future and your wallet. The best part? We’re offering full scholarships to cover the entire $895 program cost. This is our way of ensuring that every ambitious student, regardless of financial circumstances, has access to the life-changing insights that come from genuine career exploration.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the Career Exploration Summer Camp, you’ll leave with far more than just a certificate. You’ll have concrete answers to the questions that matter most.

Discover Who You Are

  • Uncover your natural strengths through fun and insightful assessments.
  • Find out what motivates you, what lights you up, and what drains you.
  • See how your personality fits with different careers so you can play to your strengths.
  • Gain real self-awareness that makes future choices easier and smarter.

Explore Future-Proof Careers

  • Check out high-growth careers that match your vibe.
  • Learn what it really takes: education, skills, salary, and daily life in each job.
  • No more guessing Get the real deal, not just what your friends or TikTok say.
  • See the ROI of different paths: college, trades, military, or entrepreneurship.

Hear From People Doing The Work

  • Day 1: Young professionals share what it’s like just starting out.
  • Day 2: Mid-career pros talk about growth and challenges.
  • Day 3: Executives and entrepreneurs show you what’s possible.
  • Ask real questions and get honest answers from real people.

Build Your Game Plan

  • Walk away with a career roadmap tailored to YOU.
  • Know exactly what to do next: courses, clubs, work experience, internships.
  • Turn stress into strategy: you’ll have a plan and a purpose.
  • Start making moves now that will actually matter later.

Program Overview

Our Career Exploration Summer Camp is a comprehensive, three-day intensive program designed to jumpstart your journey of self-discovery. Unlike traditional summer camps that focus solely on academic subjects, this program is structured around you, your interests, your strengths, and your unique potential.

Program Format

  • Duration: 3 Full Days (9:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. daily)
  • Target Audience: Rising sophomores, juniors, and seniors (ages 15–17)
  • Location: Hosted on or near local college campuses (specific locations available by January 1, 2026)
  • Setting: Each day includes interactive workshops, one-on-one coaching sessions, group discussions, and hands-on activities that bring career exploration to life.

Scholarship Details

We’re thrilled to offer full scholarships to exceptional high school students who demonstrate a genuine commitment to exploring their future.

Scholarship Eligibility: All rising sophomores, juniors, and seniors (ages 15–17) are welcome to apply. While we offer full scholarships to qualified candidates, we also have the option to discuss alternative arrangements for families with specific financial circumstances.

What’s Included:

  • Three full days of expert-led instruction and career exploration
  • Access to interactive assessments and career exploration tools
  • One-on-one coaching sessions with career advisors
  • Personalized Career Roadmap document
  • Certificate of Completion
  • All materials and resources provided during the program

Benefits for Students Like You

Self-Discovery That Leads to Purpose-Driven Decisions

Too many high school students drift through their years without real clarity about their direction. This program changes that. By understanding your authentic interests and strengths, you’ll make decisions about classes, activities, and colleges that align with your true self, not what you think you “should” do.

Expert Guidance in a Supportive Environment

Our advisors are not just knowledgeable; they’re trained to meet students where they are and help them see possibilities they may not have considered. You’ll be guided by professionals who have dedicated their careers to helping students like you succeed.

Real-World Career Insights

Forget outdated career stereotypes. You’ll learn what real careers look like, the education paths that lead to them, and how to evaluate whether a particular path is right for you. This knowledge is invaluable as you approach college selection.

Networking with Peers Who Share Your Drive

One often-overlooked benefit of the summer camp is the community you’ll join. You’ll meet other ambitious students, exchange ideas, and build connections with people who understand the pressure and excitement of planning your future.

A Practical Roadmap You Can Actually Use

Theory is fine, but what matters is action. Your personalized Career Roadmap gives you specific next steps to take throughout the rest of high school, making it easier to stay on track and make progress toward your goals.

Peace of Mind for Your Family

This program isn’t just for you; it benefits your entire family. When students have clarity about their direction, families experience less stress around college and career decisions. Your parents will appreciate the confidence and focus the program provides.

Eligibility Criteria

Who Should Apply?

Our program is ideal for you if you:

  • Are a rising sophomore, junior, or senior (ages 15–17)
  • Are curious about your future and interested in exploring different career possibilities
  • Want to understand your natural strengths and how they connect to career options
  • Are looking for clarity on what comes next, whether that's college selection, course choices, or activities to pursue
  • Are ready to take ownership of your future and make intentional decisions
  • Believe that early exploration and planning lead to better outcomes

You don’t need to be a straight A student or have it all figured out. In fact, students who feel uncertain about their direction often benefit the most from this program. What matters is your willingness to engage, reflect, and take action.

Application Requirements:

To apply for the Career Exploration Summer Camp Scholarship, you’ll need to:

  • Complete the Application Form: Tell us a bit about yourself, your interests, and what you hope to get from the program.
  • Submit an Essay: Write a short essay (250-500 words) explaining why you're interested in attending the Career Exploration Summer Camp in 2026. Share what questions you have about your future, what you're curious about, and why you believe this program would be valuable for you.

How to Apply

Ready to take control of your future? Applying is simple and straightforward.

Step 1:

Our team will review all applications and notify you of the scholarship decision. Successful applicants will receive details about program logistics, including specific campus location, final schedule, and next steps.
